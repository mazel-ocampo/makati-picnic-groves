🌳 Picnic Spots Worth Visiting

Looking for a little break from the busy city? Here are some green spaces around Makati where you can relax, enjoy a picnic, take a walk, or simply spend some quiet time outdoors.

🌳 Salcedo Park

⭐ 4.5/5

🕒 Open daily

A popular green space in the heart of Makati. Salcedo Park is a relaxing place for a picnic, morning walk, casual hangout, or a peaceful break surrounded by the city.

🌿 Paco Park

⭐ 4.4/5

🕒 Daytime visits

A historic and peaceful garden surrounded by greenery. Paco Park offers a quieter atmosphere away from the busy streets and is a lovely place for a slow walk and a relaxing outdoor visit.

🌱 Washington SyCip Park

⭐ 4.5/5

🕒 Open daily

A beautifully maintained green space in Makati with plenty of plants, trees, and peaceful corners. It is a great choice when you want somewhere calm to sit, walk, or enjoy a quiet outdoor break.

🌳 Ayala Triangle Gardens

⭐ 4.6/5

🕒 Open daily

A well-known urban garden surrounded by Makati's skyscrapers. With its open spaces and greenery, it is a convenient spot for walking, relaxing, meeting friends, or enjoying a quick picnic.
