💚 About Me

Hi! I'm Mazel Ocampo, and I created Makati Picnic Groves as a simple guide for people who want to discover relaxing green spaces around the city.

I chose this topic because Makati is often associated with busy streets, offices, shopping centers, and skyscrapers. However, there are also peaceful parks and gardens where people can slow down, enjoy some fresh air, and spend time with friends or family.

My recommendations focus on places that are enjoyable for picnics, walks, casual hangouts, and quiet breaks. I included details such as location information, opening hours, ratings, and descriptions to make it easier to decide which place might suit your visit.

I hope this guide helps visitors discover the greener and more relaxing side of Makati. 🌳✨
