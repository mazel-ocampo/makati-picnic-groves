About This Guide

A simple guide to discovering relaxing green spaces and parks in and around Makati.

About Me

Hi! I'm Mazel, the creator of Makati Picnic Groves. I created this guide to share interesting outdoor places where people can relax, walk around, enjoy nature, and spend time away from busy surroundings.

The goal of this website is to make it easier for visitors to discover green spaces and learn useful information before visiting.

Why This Topic?

Parks and green spaces provide people with places to relax, exercise, socialize, and enjoy nature even in busy urban areas. Makati has several places that offer these experiences.

This guide brings some of these places together in one simple website so visitors can compare them and choose a place that matches what they are looking for.

Why Trust These Recommendations?

The information in this guide is based on available descriptions, visitor information, location details, and general observations about each place.

Visitors are still encouraged to check the latest schedules, rules, and conditions before visiting.
