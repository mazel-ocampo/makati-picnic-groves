A Peaceful Green Escape in Makati

Ayala Triangle Park is a relaxing green space surrounded by Makati's busy city buildings. It is a great place to take a break, sit under the trees, enjoy a picnic, or simply spend quiet time outdoors.

I recommend this park because it gives visitors a peaceful place to relax while still being located in the middle of Makati. It is especially nice for casual meetups, short walks, and enjoying some fresh air.
