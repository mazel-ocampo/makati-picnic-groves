Ayala Triangle Park

A green urban escape in the middle of Makati's busy business district.

About the Place

Ayala Triangle Park is a popular green space located in the Makati Central Business District. It provides visitors with open areas, shaded walkways, trees, art installations, and nearby restaurants and cafes.

It is a good place for walking, relaxing, taking photographs, meeting friends, or enjoying a short outdoor break.

Place Details

Type Urban Park
Price Free
