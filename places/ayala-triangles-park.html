Ayala Triangles Park

Ayala Triangles Park is a refreshing green space in the middle of Makati's busy business district. The park gives visitors a chance to slow down, take a walk, sit among the greenery, and enjoy a peaceful outdoor break while still being surrounded by the city.

I recommend this place for anyone who wants a convenient escape from the busy streets. It is a nice spot for a relaxing walk, a casual meet-up, taking photographs, or simply enjoying some fresh air during the day.

✨ Place Details

Location Makati City, Metro Manila
Opening Hours 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM
Entrance Fee Free
Best Time to Visit Late afternoon or early evening
Recommended For Walking, relaxing, photography, and casual meet-ups
Rating ⭐ 4.6/5
