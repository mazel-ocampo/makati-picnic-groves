Washington SyCip Park

A calm and beautifully maintained green space in Legazpi Village.

About the Place

Washington SyCip Park is a quiet green space located in Legazpi Village, Makati City. The park features mature trees, benches, shaded areas, a koi pond, and peaceful surroundings.

It is a nice place for walking, relaxing, meeting friends, or simply taking a short break from the busy city environment.
