Paco Park

Paco Park is a historic garden and former cemetery that offers a peaceful atmosphere away from the busiest parts of Manila. Its circular layout, greenery, and historic surroundings give it a different feeling from a typical city park.

I recommend Paco Park for visitors who enjoy quiet walks, history, architecture, and relaxing outdoor spaces. It is a good choice when you want somewhere calm and reflective rather than a busy recreational park.

✨ Place Details

Location Paco, Manila
Opening Hours 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM
Entrance Fee Small entrance fee may apply
Best Time to Visit Morning or late afternoon
Recommended For Quiet walks, history, photography, and relaxing
Rating ⭐ 4.5/5
