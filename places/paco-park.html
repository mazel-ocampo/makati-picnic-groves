Paco Park

A peaceful historic garden surrounded by greenery and heritage.

About the Place

Paco Park is a historic garden and former cemetery located in Paco, Manila. It is known for its circular walls, peaceful garden areas, chapel, and historic atmosphere.

Visitors can enjoy walking around the gardens, taking photographs, learning about its history, and spending time in a quiet outdoor environment.

Place Details

Location Paco, Manila
Type Historic Park and Garden
Price Free
Best For History, photography, walking, relaxing
