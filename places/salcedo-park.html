Salcedo Park

A convenient green space for relaxing and enjoying a peaceful outdoor break.

About the Place

Salcedo Park, also known as Jaime C. Velasquez Park, is located in Salcedo Village, Makati City. It offers open grassy areas, walking paths, trees, and spaces where visitors can relax.

The park is also known for the Salcedo Saturday Market, making it a popular weekend destination for food, shopping, and outdoor activities.
